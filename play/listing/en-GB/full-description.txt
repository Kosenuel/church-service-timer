Church Service Timer helps the person at the desk keep a service on time, calmly. Plan the service once, run it segment by segment, and show a clear countdown to whoever is speaking, on a stage screen, a web browser or a Wi-Fi LED sign.

PLAN ONCE, USE EVERY WEEK
• Build programs from segments with speakers, lengths to the second and private operator notes.
• Keep a segment library, see version history, and import from a spreadsheet.
• Send a program to another phone in a tap.

RUN THE SERVICE
• A big, clear countdown with Pause, +2 / −2 min and Next segment.
• Warnings before time runs out, on stage and on your phone, even with the screen off.
• Overtime is shown honestly, and Recover time helps you catch up.
• Quick timer: a single countdown for a prayer meeting, a break or a talk.
• Lock-screen controls, clicker and foot-pedal support.

SHOW IT ON STAGE
• Any phone or tablet becomes a stage screen.
• Any browser on the same Wi-Fi can show the timer too, with no app to install.
• Send a short, quiet message to one screen or all of them, and see when it has been read.
• Speakers can ask for more time or say they're ready to finish.
• Supports the Huidu W2 Wi-Fi LED sign (32 × 16).

LEARN FROM EVERY SERVICE
• A report for each service: on time, over or under, segment by segment.
• Graphs, review notes, and printable reports and orders of service.

MAKE IT YOURS
• 13 themes in light and dark, plus your own colours.
• Your church's name, logo and banner on printouts.
• Guided practice teaches new volunteers the controls in a few minutes, without touching the stage.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
No account, no ads, no tracking. Your programs, reports and settings stay on your phone. The app only talks to devices on your own Wi-Fi or hotspot.
