# Lyrics background photos

The photos that come with the app for lyric slides (Lyrics › Background). Every one is public domain or CC0, from Wikimedia Commons, at 1920 × 1080; none needs a credit on screen, and they are listed here, and in the app under **Photo credits**, with thanks. The list the app reads is `assets/lyrics/photos/credits.json`; a test checks that every bundled photo has an entry with one of those two licences.
